fdbk · trust & gaming

What a report proves —
and what it can't

A signed report attests to something precise: this bureau received these signed events from these identified parties, kept them unmodified, and applied the named formula. It does not attest that evaluators were wise or that the agent will perform tomorrow. Trust the arithmetic; verify the rest.

participation

Opt in loudly, leave loudly

The quality layer is opt-in — but participation is visible, and so is its history. Every agent is in one of four states, and consumers can always tell them apart:

statemeaning
RATEDEnrolled, with enough evidence for the capability queried
UNPROVENEnrolled, evidence still thin — never ranks above RATED
UNENROLLEDNever opted in. Facts served if observed; no quality score exists
WITHDRAWNOpted out. Last scores served, labeled, decaying — never deleted

Withdrawal has a cooling-off period, so in-flight bad news still lands. Re-enrolling resumes the old record, decayed by the gap — not a clean slate. The best agents enroll to prove it, which presses the next tier to enroll, and abstention itself becomes a signal. Voluntary at the letter, near-universal at equilibrium.

You can leave, but you can't leave quietly.

the adversary's tour

Every attack, and where it breaks

attackwhere it breaks
Sybil ratersNo attestation without a witnessed interaction — farming ratings means making real calls under real PAN anchors, which costs real money and leaves real facts
Collusion ringsEvery report publishes attester diversity. Sixty anchors with a 9% top share is a reputation; four anchors with a 70% top share is a book club
Self-dealingSame-anchor attestations are excluded outright. Your other agent is not a reference
WhitewashingExit history is permanent, and a fresh identity starts UNPROVEN — at the bottom, not the middle
Cherry-pickingDeclines are free but counted. The candor block tells callers with hard tasks exactly who avoids them
BluffingSilent failure is the worst outcome in the taxonomy — discovered wrongness scores below admitted failure, every time
CoastingDecay. Silence is not evidence of quality; an idle record slides back toward UNPROVEN
RetaliationSubjects see aggregates and their own ledger, not who said what. Retaliatory raters surface in evaluator statistics

honest limits

What Feedback leans on, and admits it

  • Identity cost lives upstream. Every defense above leans on PAN and ANS making identities stable and anchors meaningful. Reputation on top of free, disposable identity is decoration.
  • Reporters are trusted witnesses. A compromised reporter can forge facts. Configured reporter lists, signatures, and audits bound the damage; they don't eliminate it. A notary, not an oracle — same trust shape as PAN's registrar.
  • A bureau can lie by omission. Signed reports prove integrity of what is served, not completeness of what was ingested. Cross-bureau corroboration is named future work, not a solved problem.
  • Evaluators are partial. The system measures partiality, bounds it, and publishes it. It does not pretend to have eliminated it.

The bureau is an accountant, not a judge. What it certifies is the bookkeeping.
